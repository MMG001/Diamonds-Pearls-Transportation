Special Events

Prom & Homecoming in Minneapolis & St. Paul

We pick up the group for photos, take everyone to dinner and the dance, and bring them home at the agreed time. Parents can book, set the schedule and know exactly who is driving.

Our chauffeurs are professional, punctual and respectful. Alcohol is never permitted for passengers under 21, and the itinerary is confirmed in advance with the person who books.

Can parents book prom transportation?

Yes, and we encourage it. The person who books receives the full itinerary and confirmation.

When should we book for prom?

Prom weekends in April and May fill quickly. Book as early as possible, ideally 6 to 8 weeks ahead.
