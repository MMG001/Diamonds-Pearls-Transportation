About the Vehicle

Room, Comfort and a Polished Arrival

The Suburban is the vehicle behind every Diamond & Pearls ride. It is a full-size SUV with generous room for passengers and luggage, a quiet cabin and a commanding, elegant presence at the curb.

Clients book it for MSP and private jet transfers, executive travel between meetings, wedding days, proms and evenings out. You ride in the back; your chauffeur handles the route, the timing and the doors.

Service is by the hour with a two-hour minimum. Tell us your date, times and stops and we will confirm availability and send your quote.

Suburban Questions

What vehicle will I be riding in?

Every ride is in our black 2026 Chevrolet Suburban, a full-size luxury SUV.

How much does the Suburban cost per hour?

Standard service is $90 per hour and includes airport transportation. Corporate service is $120 per hour and includes private jet transportation.

Is there a minimum booking?

All bookings require a minimum of two hours.

Can I book the Suburban for an airport pickup?

Yes. Airport transportation is included in the standard rate, and every airport pickup includes flight tracking.

Can I book the Suburban for a wedding or special event?

Yes. The Suburban is available for weddings, proms, anniversaries, nights out and other special events.

What if my group needs more room?

Call us for group rates and we will plan the right number of trips to keep everyone on time.
