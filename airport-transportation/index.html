Frequently Asked Questions

How far ahead should I book an airport ride?

We recommend booking at least 24 hours ahead. For early-morning flights, holidays and large groups, book as early as you can so we can reserve the right vehicle.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

Your chauffeur tracks your flight and adjusts the pickup time automatically, so you are met when you actually land.

Do you offer meet-and-greet at MSP?

Yes. Your chauffeur can meet you inside the terminal at baggage claim and help with your luggage to the vehicle.
