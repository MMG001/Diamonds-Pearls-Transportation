Our Story

Built on Polish and Reliability

Diamond & Pearls Luxury Transportation was founded with a simple idea: every ride should feel as refined as the occasion it serves. Like a diamond and a pearl, we believe great service combines strength with grace.

We serve executives, families, couples and groups across Minneapolis, St. Paul and the Twin Cities metro, with a focus on punctuality, discretion and genuine hospitality.
