Wine Tasting Tours in Minneapolis & St. Paul

Minnesota wineries are known for cold-hardy grapes developed for the northern climate. Popular routes head east toward Stillwater and the St. Croix Valley or west and south of the metro.

We recommend three to four wineries over a five-hour tour, with time for a lunch stop.

  • Three to four winery stops
  • Custom or suggested routes
  • Lunch stop on request
  • Ideal for bachelorettes and birthdays
