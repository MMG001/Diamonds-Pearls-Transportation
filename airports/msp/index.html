MSP

Transportation at Minneapolis-St. Paul International Airport

MSP is the region's main airport and a major Delta hub. Terminal 1 serves most airlines, while Terminal 2 serves Sun Country and several other carriers. We confirm your terminal before every pickup.

For arrivals, your chauffeur tracks your flight and can meet you at baggage claim. For departures, we build in time for traffic, weather and security.

  • Terminal 1 and Terminal 2
  • Flight tracking
  • Baggage claim meet-and-greet
  • Hotel and downtown transfers

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you track flights at MSP?

Yes. Every airport pickup includes flight tracking so your chauffeur adjusts to your actual arrival time.

How do I book a ride to or from MSP?

Request a quote online with your flight details, pickup address and number of passengers. We will confirm your ride and send your chauffeur's details before pickup.
