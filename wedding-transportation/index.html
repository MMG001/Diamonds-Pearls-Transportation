Frequently Asked Questions

How early should we book wedding transportation?

Peak wedding dates in Minnesota book up early. We recommend reserving 3 to 6 months ahead, especially for Saturdays from May through October.

Can you work with our wedding planner?

Absolutely. We are happy to coordinate directly with your planner or venue coordinator on timing and pickup locations.

Do you transport guests between the ceremony and reception?

Yes. Guest shuttle service can run between hotels, the ceremony and the reception venue on a schedule you choose.
